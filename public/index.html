About me

A little about how I got here.

I'm a 23-year-old developer and marketer based in Windsor, Ontario. I started building websites when I was 9 - it was the first thing that really stuck. From there I got into game development, eventually co-founding a small studio (85 Plus Games) with a popular streamer who funded the team, and leading the 5-person crew that shipped Unrooted on Steam, where it sits at Very Positive today. That's where I learned to think in systems, ship things end-to-end, and obsess over how something feels to use.

Marketing came later. I picked it up almost by accident, got hooked on the strategy side of it, and pretty quickly realized the most interesting work happens at the intersection - where the campaigns, the software, and the data all meet. That's the lane I work in now: building the marketing systems and writing the software underneath them, usually on the same project.

I've worked across automotive retail, local services, gaming, and SaaS - because the underlying work transfers. If a business runs on leads, conversations, and follow-up, I can help.

A few campaigns I've run

The kinds of things I like to design.

Most of my favourite campaigns are simple ideas executed end-to-end. The examples below are from automotive, but the structure works in any business that takes inbound leads.

01

Budget & fit finder

Visitors enter what they can afford and I show them options that match. Small idea, but it changes the kind of leads you get. Works for cars, homes, services, anything priced.

02

Spin-to-win capture

A compliant promo wheel I built to collect phone numbers without feeling spammy. It funnels straight into the SMS system. Useful any time you need a low-friction opt-in.

03

Invite-only sale

Gated, expiring-password campaigns that make people feel like insiders. Reply rates went up, and so did intent. Works for any limited-time offer.

04

Lifecycle re-engagement

Reaching out to past customers at the right point in their cycle - automated, personalized, and surprisingly effective. Trade-ins, renewals, refills, anything cyclical.
